Sebastian Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

"Sebastian" (the "App") is an AI butler app that lets you create calendar events and reminders just by speaking or typing. The developer and operator of this App (the "Operator") takes your privacy seriously. The App does not require any account registration or sign‑in, and does not collect personal information such as your name, contact details, or location. Text parsing is performed using the cloud AI service Google Gemini, regardless of device type or subscription status (see below). Nothing is sent until you give in‑app consent beforehand. In addition, the App sends anonymous usage statistics to the Operator's own server to help improve the App (you can turn this off at any time — see "3‑3. Anonymous usage statistics"). Details follow below.

1. Information we do NOT collect

The App does not collect, transmit, or store the following:

  • Personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number (there is no account/sign‑in system at all)
  • Location data
  • Contacts, photos, or other on‑device data
  • The contents of events/reminders already saved in your calendar (see Section 4)
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA) or any information used to track you across other companies' apps or websites

Only two kinds of data are ever sent: ① the text you type or speak, for chat parsing (only after consent — Sections 2–3), and ② anonymous usage statistics for app improvement (Section 3‑3).

2. How text is parsed

Parsing of text you type into the chat (extracting the event title, date/time, and alert) works the same way for every device and for both free and paid users:

  • Cloud parsing (all devices, free and Pro alike): Only the text you typed is sent, via the Operator's relay server (Supabase), to Google's Gemini API, and the parsed result is returned (see Section 3 below). Most requests are handled on the free tier of the Gemini API; if that tier is congested or its limit is reached, the Operator's own pay‑as‑you‑go backup key may be used instead (the destination, content, and handling are identical either way). "Sebastian Pro" subscribers get a higher daily number of parses, but use the exact same Gemini service as free users.
    Note: earlier versions used on‑device parsing, and a different AI service for Pro subscribers only. To keep accuracy consistent, since September 30, 2026 every device and plan uses Google Gemini exclusively.
  • Without parsing: If you do not consent to the above, or if you are offline, you can still use every feature of the App by adding and editing events/reminders manually.

3. About cloud parsing (Google Gemini)

Whenever chat parsing is used, the text you typed is sent to Google's servers, regardless of your device or subscription status. Because this is not an on‑device process, we disclose the following:

  • We ask for your in‑app consent before anything is sent. The first time cloud parsing is needed, an in‑app screen clearly states what is sent (only the text you typed) and where it goes (the Google Gemini API), and asks for your consent. Nothing is sent until you consent. You can withdraw consent at any time from Settings > External AI Parsing (Cloud) — after withdrawing, nothing more is sent.
  • Only the text to be parsed is sent. We never send your name, contacts, calendar contents, or location.
  • Most parsing happens on Google's free tier (Gemini API free usage tier). Under the free tier's terms, submitted content may be used by Google to improve its services and models. Please avoid entering sensitive information. If the free tier is congested or its limit is reached, the Operator's own pay‑as‑you‑go backup key may be used, but the destination, content, and purpose remain unchanged. Google's handling of this data is governed by the Gemini API Additional Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • "Sebastian Pro" subscribers are sent to the same Google Gemini API. Subscribers simply get more parses per day than free users — what is sent, where it goes, and how it is handled are identical.
  • To stay within the free tier and manage usage limits, the relay server assigns an anonymous random ID per device. This ID cannot be used to identify you personally.
  • The Operator does not store, analyze, or share your text with any third party beyond relaying it for parsing.
  • If you don't want to use cloud parsing, choose "Not now" on the consent screen, or turn it off from Settings > External AI Parsing (Cloud). You can still add events manually from the "+" button without using chat, with full functionality (and in that case nothing is ever sent externally).

3-2. Help improve parsing accuracy (optional, off by default)

The App has an optional feature that helps gradually improve parsing accuracy for everyone. It is only active if you explicitly turn on the "Help improve parsing" toggle inside Settings > External AI Parsing (Cloud). It is off by default, and nothing related to this feature is ever sent while it is off.

  • What is sent: Only when you correct and re‑save an AI parsing result do we send three pieces of data: the text you typed, the AI's original (incorrect) result, and the corrected result you confirmed. If the AI was already correct and you made no correction, nothing is sent.
  • Anonymity: We never send your name, contacts, location, or raw device ID. For rough aggregate statistics only, we use a one‑way, irreversible hash of the device ID, which cannot be used to identify you.
  • How it's used: Collected corrections are used only to give the AI "good examples" or "things to avoid" for similar future inputs, improving accuracy and reducing repeated mistakes. This is sent to the same destination as parsing (Google Gemini API).
  • You can stop at any time by turning the same toggle off — nothing more will be sent afterward.

3-3. Anonymous usage statistics (for app improvement)

To understand which features are used and how, in order to improve the App, we send the following anonymous data to the Operator's own server (Supabase). This is a separate mechanism from the AI parsing above, and none of it is sent to any external AI service.

  • Event names (e.g. app launch, event registered) and their timestamps
  • Session length and time of day the App was used
  • App version and iOS version
  • An anonymous install ID (a random string generated the first time you launch the App; it is never linked to your name, email, device ID, or advertising ID, and changes if you delete and reinstall the App)

This never includes any text you typed, the contents of your events/reminders, dates/times, or location. We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and we do not use advertising identifiers.

You can turn this off at any time from Settings > "Send usage data" (toggle "Send anonymous usage statistics" off). Once off, nothing more is sent, and any not‑yet‑sent data is discarded.

3-4. Sharing your schedule (optional feature)

When you open the Sharing tab, the app automatically creates a per-device account for this feature — no sign-up, login, name, or email address is requested or required.

When you join or create a group, we store the following on our server (Supabase):

  • The display name you enter when joining a group (any nickname you choose)
  • For each calendar you select to share: the event's start/end time, whether it's an all-day event, and either the event title, or — if you choose the "busy only" visibility option for that group — just a "busy" marker instead of the title
  • The event's calendar color
  • An anonymous per-device account ID used to tell group members apart

This information is visible only to members of the same group. We never send location, notes, or attendee information. You can mark any individual event "don't share this event," and it will be excluded.

If you never open the Sharing tab, no data for this feature is ever sent.

You can delete this data at any time:

  • Settings > Other > "Delete Sharing Account" removes you from every group you've joined and deletes your stored events and display name from our server
  • To leave just one group, use "Leave Group" in that group's settings screen

When you leave or delete your account, your events also disappear from other members' views immediately.

4. Access to your Calendar and Reminders

The App requests permission to access the iOS built‑in Calendar / Reminders (EventKit) so it can create, edit, and display events and reminders you have approved.

  • This data is stored only on your device and in your own iCloud (Apple) account.
  • The Operator of this App has no ability to view, retrieve, or transmit this data.
  • Events and reminders are only ever created after you review and approve them on a confirmation card.
  • You can change or revoke this permission at any time from the iOS Settings app.

5. Third parties & advertising

The App does not include any advertising SDKs or third‑party tracking SDKs. We never share your data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes. Data leaves the App only in two ways: ① text you typed is sent to Google (Gemini API) for the sole purpose of parsing it, after you've consented in‑app (Sections 2–3); ② anonymous usage statistics are sent to the Operator's own server to improve the App (Section 3‑3 — this is not a third‑party disclosure).

6. Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, please contact us by email at dr.qururi+sebastian@gmail.com.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be changed without prior notice. Important changes will be announced within the App.
