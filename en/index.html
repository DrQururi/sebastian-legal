Sebastian Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

"Sebastian" (the "App") is an AI butler app that lets you create calendar events and reminders just by speaking or typing. The developer and operator of this App (the "Operator") takes your privacy seriously. The App does not require any account registration or sign‑in, and does not collect personal information such as your name, contact details, or location. Text parsing is performed using a cloud AI service, regardless of device type or subscription status — Anthropic's Claude in version 1.9.3 and later, Google's Gemini in version 1.9.2 and earlier (see below). Nothing is sent until you give in‑app consent beforehand. In addition, the App sends anonymous usage statistics to the Operator's own server to help improve the App (you can turn this off at any time — see "3‑3. Anonymous usage statistics"). Details follow below.

1. Information we do NOT collect

The App does not collect, transmit, or store the following:

  • Personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number (there is no account/sign‑in system at all)
  • Location data
  • Contacts, your photo library, or other on‑device data (the only exception is the images you choose yourself in "Read events from images", which are loaded temporarily to be converted to text on your device; they are not stored and the image itself is never sent — Section 2‑2)
  • The contents of events/reminders already saved in your calendar (see Section 4)
  • Advertising identifiers (IDFA) or any information used to track you across other companies' apps or websites

Only two kinds of data are ever sent: ① the text you type or speak, or text read from an image on your device, for chat parsing (only after consent — Sections 2, 2‑2 and 3), and ② anonymous usage statistics for app improvement (Section 3‑3).

2. How text is parsed

Parsing of text you type into the chat (extracting the event title, date/time, and alert) works the same way for every device and for both free and paid users:

  • Cloud parsing (all devices, free and Pro alike): Only the text you typed (or, for image import, the text read from the image on your device) is sent, via the Operator's relay server (Supabase), to a cloud AI service, and the parsed result is returned (see Section 3 below). The destination depends on the App version: version 1.9.3 and later send only to Anthropic's Claude (nothing is sent to Google), and version 1.9.2 and earlier send to Google's Gemini API. "Sebastian Pro" subscribers get a higher number of parses, but use the same AI service as free users.
    Note: earlier versions used on‑device parsing, and a different AI service for Pro subscribers only. Since September 30, 2026, version 1.9.2 and earlier use Google Gemini on every device and plan, and version 1.9.3 and later use Anthropic (Claude) instead.
  • Without parsing: If you do not consent to the above, or if you are offline, you can still use every feature of the App by adding and editing events/reminders manually.

2‑2. Reading events from images (text is read on your device; images are not sent)

With the image button in the Chat screen you can choose photos, screenshots, or image files (up to 3 at a time), and the App lists the events it finds in them as candidates.

  • Converting the image to text happens on your device (using Apple's text recognition). The image itself is never sent to the Operator, Google, or Anthropic, and is not stored (only a small thumbnail is kept in memory to display in the chat).
  • What is sent is only the text read from the image on your device. It goes to the same destination as cloud parsing in Section 3 (Anthropic in 1.9.3 and later, Google in 1.9.2 and earlier) and requires the same consent: nothing is sent before you consent or after you withdraw. If you had already consented before this feature existed, you are asked once, the first time you import an image.
  • Photos are chosen with the standard iOS photo picker, so the App does not ask for access to your whole photo library; only the images you pick are loaded. The same applies to "Choose from Files", "Use image from clipboard", and drag & drop — only images you deliberately provide are used.
  • There is a limit (6,000 characters) on how much text is sent per request; anything beyond it is not sent.
  • If an image shows other people's names or similar, that text may be included in the recognized text and sent. Please take care with images containing other people's information.

3. About cloud parsing (Anthropic Claude / Google Gemini)

Whenever chat parsing is used, the text you typed (or, for image import, the text read from your image on your device) is sent to an external AI service's servers, regardless of your device or subscription status. Because this is not an on‑device process, we disclose the following:

  • The destination depends on the App version. Version 1.9.3 and later: Anthropic's Claude only (nothing is sent to Google). Version 1.9.2 and earlier: Google's Gemini API. In version 1.9.3 we changed the AI parsing destination from Google (Gemini) to Anthropic (Claude).
  • We ask for your in‑app consent before anything is sent. The first time cloud parsing is needed, an in‑app screen clearly states what is sent (only the text you typed, or text read from an image on your device; the image itself is never sent) and where it goes (in 1.9.3 and later, Anthropic's Claude), and asks for your consent. Nothing is sent until you consent. If you consented to AI parsing in version 1.9.2 or earlier, you will be asked again, once, the first time you use AI parsing after updating to 1.9.3, because the destination has changed. If you decline, nothing is sent and chat AI parsing is unavailable, but the rest of the App — including adding and editing events manually — keeps working. You can withdraw consent at any time in the App's Settings (Settings > AI Parsing (Claude) in 1.9.3 and later; Settings > AI Parsing (Google Gemini) in 1.9.2 and earlier) — after withdrawing, nothing more is sent.
  • Only the text to be parsed is sent (for image import, the text read from the image on your device). The image itself is never sent. We never send the name, contacts, calendar contents, or location that the App collects from your device. Note that text visible in an image (for example another person's name) may be sent as text so that events can be read from it.
  • [1.9.3 and later: Anthropic (Claude)] According to Anthropic's published information (Privacy Center), inputs and outputs received through its API are not used to train its models by default (Is my data used for model training?). Anthropic also states that, for its API, inputs and outputs are automatically deleted within 30 days of receipt or generation, except where retention is needed to enforce its usage policy or is required by law (How long do you store my organization’s data?). Anthropic's handling of data is governed by its Privacy Policy and Commercial Terms. Anthropic's published information may change.
  • [1.9.2 and earlier: Google (Gemini)] Most parsing happens on Google's free tier (Gemini API free usage tier). Under the free tier's terms, submitted content may be used by Google to improve its services and models. Please avoid entering sensitive information. If the free tier is congested or its limit is reached, the Operator's own pay‑as‑you‑go backup key may be used, but the destination, content, and purpose remain unchanged. Google's handling of this data is governed by the Gemini API Additional Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
  • "Sebastian Pro" subscribers are sent to the same destination as free users for their App version. Subscribers simply get more parses than free users — what is sent, where it goes, and how it is handled are identical.
  • To manage usage limits, the relay server assigns an anonymous random ID per device. This ID cannot be used to identify you personally.
  • The Operator does not store, analyze, or share your text with any third party beyond relaying it for parsing.
  • If you don't want to use cloud parsing, choose "Not now" on the consent screen, or turn it off in Settings (AI Parsing (Claude) in 1.9.3 and later). You can still add events manually from the "+" button without using chat, with full functionality (and in that case nothing is ever sent externally).

3-2. Help improve parsing accuracy (optional, off by default)

The App has an optional feature that helps gradually improve parsing accuracy for everyone. It is only active if you explicitly turn on the "Help improve parsing" toggle inside the App's Settings (AI Parsing (Claude) in 1.9.3 and later; AI Parsing (Google Gemini) in 1.9.2 and earlier). It is off by default, and nothing related to this feature is ever sent while it is off.

  • What is sent: Only when you correct and re‑save an AI parsing result do we send three pieces of data: the text you typed (or the text read from an image), the AI's original (incorrect) result, and the corrected result you confirmed. If the AI was already correct and you made no correction, nothing is sent.
  • Anonymity: We never send your name, contacts, location, or raw device ID. For rough aggregate statistics only, we use a one‑way, irreversible hash of the device ID, which cannot be used to identify you.
  • How it's used: Collected corrections are used only to give the AI "good examples" or "things to avoid" for similar future inputs, improving accuracy and reducing repeated mistakes. In 1.9.2 and earlier, this goes to the same destination as parsing (Google Gemini API). In 1.9.3 and later, corrections are stored anonymously on the Operator's server and are not sent to Google (in 1.9.3 you are also asked again whether to turn this feature on; it is off by default).
  • You can stop at any time by turning the same toggle off — nothing more will be sent afterward.

3-3. Anonymous usage statistics (for app improvement)

To understand which features are used and how, in order to improve the App, we send the following anonymous data to the Operator's own server (Supabase). This is a separate mechanism from the AI parsing above, and none of it is sent to any external AI service.

  • Event names (e.g. app launch, event registered) and their timestamps
  • Session length and time of day the App was used
  • App version and iOS version
  • An anonymous install ID (a random string generated the first time you launch the App; it is never linked to your name, email, device ID, or advertising ID, and changes if you delete and reinstall the App)

This never includes any text you typed, text read from images, the contents of your events/reminders, dates/times, or location. We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and we do not use advertising identifiers.

You can turn this off at any time from Settings > "Send usage data" (toggle "Send anonymous usage statistics" off). Once off, nothing more is sent, and any not‑yet‑sent data is discarded.

3-4. Sharing your schedule (optional feature)

When you open the Sharing tab, the app automatically creates a per-device account for this feature — no sign-up, login, name, or email address is requested or required.

When you join or create a group, we store the following on our server (Supabase):

  • The display name you enter when joining a group (any nickname you choose)
  • For each calendar you select to share: the event's start/end time, whether it's an all-day event, and either the event title, or — if you choose the "busy only" visibility option for that group — just a "busy" marker instead of the title
  • The event's calendar color
  • An anonymous per-device account ID used to tell group members apart

This information is visible only to members of the same group. We never send location, notes, or attendee information. You can mark any individual event "don't share this event," and it will be excluded.

If you never open the Sharing tab, no data for this feature is ever sent.

You can delete this data at any time:

  • Settings > Other > "Delete Sharing Account" removes you from every group you've joined and deletes your stored events and display name from our server
  • To leave just one group, use "Leave Group" in that group's settings screen

When you leave or delete your account, your events also disappear from other members' views immediately.

4. Access to your Calendar and Reminders

The App requests permission to access the iOS built‑in Calendar / Reminders (EventKit) so it can create, edit, and display events and reminders you have approved.

  • This data is stored only on your device and in your own iCloud (Apple) account.
  • The Operator of this App has no ability to view, retrieve, or transmit this data.
  • Events and reminders are only ever created after you review and approve them on a confirmation card.
  • You can change or revoke this permission at any time from the iOS Settings app.

5. Third parties & advertising

The App does not include any advertising SDKs or third‑party tracking SDKs. We never share your data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes. Data leaves the App only in two ways: ① text you typed (or, for image import, text read from an image on your device — never the image itself) is sent, for the sole purpose of parsing it, to Anthropic (Claude) in 1.9.3 and later or to Google (Gemini API) in 1.9.2 and earlier, after you've consented in‑app (Sections 2, 2‑2 and 3); ② anonymous usage statistics are sent to the Operator's own server to improve the App (Section 3‑3 — this is not a third‑party disclosure).

Service description

"Sebastian" is a practical tool that parses free‑form text you enter, or text read from images on your device, (with your consent) using cloud AI, and — after you confirm the result — creates or edits events and reminders in the iOS Calendar and Reminders apps. You can also add and edit events/reminders manually at any time.

System requirements

Automatic AI parsing works via the cloud, sending your text (or text read from an image on your device) to a cloud AI service (Anthropic's Claude in 1.9.3 and later; Google's Gemini API in 1.9.2 and earlier) for every device and subscription tier. This only happens after you consent on an in‑app confirmation screen, and you can turn it off at any time from Settings (see Privacy Policy Sections 2–3). Because capacity is limited, parsing may be temporarily unavailable during busy periods or once you reach your usage limit. In that case, or if you choose not to use parsing, you can still add and edit events/reminders manually.
